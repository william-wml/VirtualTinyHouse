# Studio model destination

Copy your optimized Blender export here as `studio.glb`.

The demonstration scene works without a GLB. Click **Load Studio GLB** after adding that file.

- Export as binary glTF (`.glb`).
- Exclude unneeded master objects and diagnostic geometry.
- Preserve meaningful object/group names for component visibility controls.
- Confirm repeated fasteners and instance locations after export.
- The published GLB is downloadable by website visitors; do not include private data.
- Large GLB files may require additional optimization or hosting strategies.
